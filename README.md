Aurora Ridge is an original valley flight game: a chase and cockpit view over a seeded Alpine valley, a built vector aircraft with lit instruments, golden rings to thread, fuel and damage to manage, and a short grass-and-asphalt strip to bring her home on. Three sorties — Ring Run, Ridge Landing and Storm Courier — share one flight model with lift, stall, coordinated turns and drag, and one procedural world with a river valley, a lake, the village of Alderwick and two strips. No libraries, no network requests: plain static files under `game/`, synthesised WebAudio sound, and four real screenshots of the game running.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Arrows or WASD fly, Shift/Ctrl work the throttle, G drops the gear, F sets flaps, C switches cockpit and chase cameras, P pauses and R restarts.

## Screenshots

The four screenshots in `screenshots/` are real renders of the game in a browser, staged deterministically with `?shot=start|play|ring|land&seed=1` (see `shots.json`): the title over the parked aircraft, a ring run mid-flight, a ring pass, and the cockpit view on final approach.
